Welcome to Video Downloader & Browser ("we", "our", or "us"). We are strongly committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we process, how that information is handled, and our data protection principles when you use our mobile application (the "App").

🛡️ Privacy First Commitment Video Downloader & Browser is designed as a local-first utility. We do NOT collect, harvest, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) on external cloud servers. All personal operations, downloads, history, bookmarks, and vault items remain entirely on your device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute data minimization. When you use the App:

  • We do not require user registration, account creation, or login to use our core services.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or financial details.
  • We do not track your precise GPS location.
  • We do not transmit your browsing history, bookmarked pages, search queries, or downloaded files to any proprietary remote servers.

2. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

To provide essential downloader and browser features, the App processes and stores certain data locally in your device's isolated storage (using SQLite database and local preferences):

🌐 In-App Browser Data

Bookmarks, tabs, and temporary browsing cache/cookies required for web sessions are saved exclusively on your local device memory.

📥 Download History

Records of your completed, active, and queued downloads (URLs, file paths, file sizes, and download dates) are stored in a local SQLite database.

🔒 Private Vault

Files moved into the private vault are stored locally in secure app directories. Your vault access PIN is verified locally and never sent over the internet.

⚙️ Preferences

App settings such as theme mode, download quality selections, ad-blocker filters, and Wi-Fi preferences are stored locally.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

The App requests standard Android/iOS permissions strictly necessary for its features to function. Below is a detailed breakdown:

Permission Category Purpose & Usage
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Network Access Allows the in-app browser to load websites requested by the user, detect active network connectivity (e.g. Wi-Fi vs. Cellular), and download media streams.
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, AUDIO, IMAGES / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Device Storage Required to write downloaded videos, audios, and images to your public download directory or photo gallery, and to play back downloaded local media.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications Used in Android 13+ to show real-time download progress bar updates, download completions, and task failures.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE & WAKE_LOCK Background Service Allows large download tasks initiated by the user to complete reliably in the background without being killed prematurely by OS power management.

4. Third-Party Websites & Built-In Browser

The App includes a built-in browser engine powered by standard platform WebViews (InAppWebView). When you navigate to third-party websites or services:

  • Any data you enter into third-party websites is directly submitted to those respective third-party web services and is governed by their respective privacy policies.
  • The App includes an optional client-side ad and tracker filter to help reduce third-party tracking scripts loaded by web pages.
  • We do not monitor, record, or intercept your encrypted web sessions or login credentials.

5. YouTube & Copyright Policy Notice

⚠️ Policy & Copyright Compliance Disclaimer In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies and YouTube's Terms of Service, Video Downloader & Browser does NOT support or allow downloading content from YouTube. The App is intended only for downloading personal, royalty-free, or publicly permissible media where permitted by applicable laws and content owners.

6. Status Saver Feature

The status saver feature accesses locally cached WhatsApp/WhatsApp Business media stored on your device only when explicitly triggered by you. The App reads these local folders to allow you to preview and save selected media to your gallery. No status data is ever transmitted off your device.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Our App is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided any information through third-party web interaction within the app, please contact us so we can provide guidance on clearing local app data.

8. Data Retention, Control & Deletion

Since all user data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can clear your browsing history, cache, cookies, and bookmarks directly inside the App settings.
  • You can delete downloaded files and vault contents at any time using the App's built-in file manager or your device's file manager.
  • Uninstalling the App or choosing "Clear Data / Clear Storage" in your Android/iOS system settings permanently erases all databases, vault items, and cached data stored by the App.

9. Security

We prioritize data security by adhering to platform sandboxing standards. We store database information and vault configuration inside the app's private sandboxed directory, preventing unauthorized access from other standard apps on your device.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, technical improvements, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this web page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
